
  So... I went ahead and made another font for some reason.
  This one is monospaced, even though it has a bunch of explicitly "cursive"
  letter forms. I based this one on my handwriting which, at this point in life,
  has become so fucked-up by my personal attempts to make some of my letters look
  more unique, along with a fascination with certain calligraphies and so forth.

  My goal here was to make words look more different at a glance for potential
  use in programming. On a previous professional project at a large tech company
  (which will remain nameless) contained classes in the same package for "note"
  and "node" and frequently used these interchangably... my brain just does not
  work this way, and while I did refactor that class away it's not the only time
  I've had these issues. Plus, I use a comically small laptop to do my personal
  projects and it's just hard to fit eighty characters times three on the screen
  when I'm trying to merge a file. The capital letters use an entirely different
  design philosophy but I really like the way it looks when this font declares

  camelCaseCamelProviderFactory = new CamelCaseCamelProviderFactoryImpl();

  It's still incomplete and needs a lot of polish - I'm not happy with some of
  the particular letter forms as written, some are missing, and a few glyphs need
  special care when paired with other letters (since kerning is gernally not an
  option with monospaced fonts).

  P.S.: Who in their right mind allowed us to use "the quick brown fox jumps over
  the lazy dog" as the standard sample pangram sentence when the far superior
  "SPHINX OF BLACK QUARTZ, JUDGE MY VOW!" was right there the whole fucking time?
